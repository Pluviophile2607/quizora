Rules

RULES AND REGULATION

Participants must register in duos only.
Both members must be from the same eligible FY class.
Each team must have one team name.
Participants must report 15 minutes before the Events.
No mobile phones or external assistance allowed.
The Quiz Master’s decision is final.
Misbehaviour or cheating will result in disqualification.

01 — ELIGIBILITY

  • This event is exclusively for FY students.
  • Students from IT, Data Science, AI/ML, and BCA may participate.
  • Participants must register in duos only, with both members from the same class.

02 — REGISTRATION

  • Registration fee: ₹20 per team. Cash payment only — no UPI or online payments.
  • Register on the announced dates from 9:45 AM – 10:00 AM in Classroom 411.
  • Teams must provide the required details. Changes to team members may not be allowed after registration.

03 — EVENT CONDUCT

  • Report to the venue before the event begins and sit in your assigned place.
  • Follow all instructions from the Quizora coordinators and quizmaster. Maintain discipline throughout.
  • Disturbance, cheating, or unfair practice may result in disqualification.

04 — QUIZ RULES

  • The quiz consists of multiple rounds covering technology, IT, Data Science, AI/ML, computers, general tech knowledge, and logical thinking.
  • Questions vary in difficulty. Submit answers within the given time; late submissions may not be accepted.
  • The quizmaster's decision on answers, scoring, and rules is final.

Round 1 - MCQ WipeOut

15 MCQs | Time: 10 minutes |
Paper-based +1 for every correct answer,
no negative spaning.
Team members may discuss quietly.
Top teams qualify for Round 2.

Round 2 - Buzzer Round

First team to press the buzzer gets to answer.
+10 for correct , –5 for wrong answers.
Buzzer should be pressed only after the question is fully read.
Must answer within 5 seconds of pressing the buzzer, else –5.
Top teams move to Round 3.

Round 3 - Visual Decoding

Identify visuals: pixelated images, logos, icons, scenes.
10 seconds per visual.
+10 for correct answers, no negative spaning.
Team members may discuss within the time limit.
Top teams advance to Round 4

Round 4 - Rapid Fire Round

Back-to-back questions until the team answers incorrectly.
+5 for each correct answer.
No passing of questions.
One team representative answers.
